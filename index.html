/// ENTITY DATABASE ///
ENTITY: THE LOCUST
Designation:    Hostile / Nocturnal / Photophobic
Height:         ~3.2 meters (extended)
Behavior:       Hunts during darkness. Avoids light sources >7 lumens.
                Teleports in 2-3 block bursts when pursuing prey.
                Does NOT enter water, boats, or open trapdoors.
Frequency:      43% chance of manifestation per night cycle.
Weakness:       High-frequency emission (Signal Tower range)

First encountered in the Faded Lands. Witnesses describe a "wrong" body — limbs too long, movement that "skips frames" like corrupted video. It does not eat. It does not sleep. It hunts.

ENTITY: BLOODED EYE
Designation:    Passive / Aerial / Observational
Height:         ~1.8 meters (wingspan ~4 meters)
Behavior:       Maintains altitude of 40-60 blocks. No aggressive actions recorded.
                Pupil dilates when observing subjects. Disappears when unobserved.
                Multiple instances may focus on a single target simultaneously.
Frequency:      Unknown. Appears without pattern.
Weakness:       None documented.

The Blooded Eye does not hunt. It witnesses. Some survivors claim it appears before major events — tower activations, mass entity migrations, or the arrival of new "guests" in the Faded Lands.

Theories suggest it is not an entity at all, but a sensor — an organ of something far larger that exists beyond the fog.

/// THE FADED LANDS ///

A biome that should not exist. Grass bleached white. Trees stripped of leaves, frozen in silent agony. The fog is not weather — it is memory, the residue of whatever happened here.

Staying longer than four minutes attracts attention. Not from the fog. From what lives inside it.
